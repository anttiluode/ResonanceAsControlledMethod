04 / why earlier single-frequency results weren't decisive

A necessary identity control

For any one frequency k, the supposedly conventional fixed-width packet can be set to q = k. It then has exactly the same angular weights, launch state and receiver echo as the parabolic packet.

The mathematical difference matters only when the source must act across multiple frequencies and is constrained to share one angular width. We test precisely that condition here, rather than claim a free-wave theorem proves memory reconstruction.

05 / source, not theorem

What failure would mean

For this locked protocol, the parabolic design must outperform the optimized fixed-width control on both unfamiliar shapes to support the narrowly stated transfer hypothesis. A win on one and a loss on another means the claimed generalization fails.

This is a numerical simulation of a damped Robin/Neumann cavity, not a formal consequence of OpenAI's free Euclidean local-smoothing result. Geometry-dependent hardware emission costs and independent physical verification remain open.
