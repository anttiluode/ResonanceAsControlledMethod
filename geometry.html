Research ledger · important limits

What would constitute progress?

Success: geometry picks a smaller calibrating family with equal or better held-out accuracy, or identifies a physically grounded rule predicting which interactions must be modeled before hearing them. Failure: geometry loses to degree-first at equal calibration and acquisition budget. Both outcomes teach us about this cavity's readable memory.

There is no claim of novel wave physics, learning wall plasticity, polynomial-time general memory, or transfer of OpenAI's 2026 Euclidean local-smoothing theorem. Calibration is exhaustive over the subset of records selected, and the full 256-state oracle is an expensive diagnostic used to evaluate failure honestly. The page reports a single deterministic noise seed, not a general statistical superiority result.

Other necessary experiments: lower-memory geometry selection as K grows, independent geometries and grid sizes, instrument-energy matching, uncertainty calibration, and a local physical write rule. The previous four-bit live reader remains available as a separate experiment.
